## 1. Result

**A5 is a structural extension of standard field theory.** In the local, non-relativistic limit it reduces to the scalar treatment. In gradient-rich regimes (warp, cosmology) it yields qualitatively new physics.

## 2. A5 Formulation

```
E⃗ = E_eff · ê(Δφ, ∇Φ)
```
E_eff = π·ε·ħ·f (scalar magnitude from A4), ê = unit vector in energy flow direction.

## 3. Comparison with Standard Field Theory

**Poynting vector:** S⃗ = (1/μ₀) E⃗_EM × B⃗ — special case of A5 for EM fields.
A5 generalises to all resonance fields.

**Stress-energy tensor T^μν:** A5 makes T^00 additionally dependent on local Δφ(x):
```
T^00_A5(x) = ρ(x) · ε²(Δφ(x))   [from RT-43 AP1]
```
This is a structural extension: T^00 acquires local phase dependence.

## 4. Limiting Cases

A5 reduces to scalar standard treatment when: ε=1 everywhere, ∇Δφ=0, v≪c.

Relativistic formulation of A5 as 4-vector: **open** (direction: RT-40 extension).
