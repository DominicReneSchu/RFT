## 3. Scalarity Postulate

E is (approximately) scalar when:
1. No dominant energy flux (S⃗ ≈ 0)
2. Homogeneous distribution (∇ρ ≈ 0)
3. Non-relativistic (v ≪ c)
4. Weak gradients (∇Φ ≈ 0)
