It is the inescapable frame that contains everything without being part of what it encloses. Transposed to the cosmos: a space curved back upon itself, as general relativity permits, has no exterior and yet finite volume – complete, without gap.

The one-point compactification of the real numbers makes the principle vivid: the open number line from −∞ to +∞ is identified at its endpoints into a single point. The line becomes a circle, the open becomes closed. The infinity symbol ∞, the lemniscate, embodies this geometrically: a loop running back into itself, without beginning or end.

What holds spatially continues temporally. In the **block universe**, past, present, and future exist equally as a four-dimensional structure (A1: ψ = A·cos(kx − ωt + φ) is already a timeless block structure). The present is the observer's intersection through this block – the resonance moment of maximum coupling (A4, A6).

The result: the universe is a **resonance bubble** – closed, complete, internally coherent. Structure emerges through resonance: through coupling of field modes, through interference, and through the dynamics of the resonance integral.

### Oscillation, Coherence, and the Emergence of Structure

The resonance bubble is not a static construct. It is traversed by oscillations – field modes that couple, amplify, or cancel one another. Structure does not arise despite, but through, this dynamic.

The **coupling efficiency** ε(Δφ) = cos²(Δφ/2) is the measure of coherence itself: Δφ = 0 means complete alignment – maximum resonance, maximum energy transfer. Δφ = π means complete cancellation. The universe is therefore not uniform noise but a field with inner topology: regions of high coherence form stable structures (particles, atoms, matter); regions of low coupling hold the substrate ready for new differentiation. Order and chaos are complementary states of the same field.

## Part 2: The Formal Cycle – From μ to μ_new

The four foundational equations of the RFT describe not only a static state but a **cyclical process**. This cycle is not a speculative assumption but a **formal consequence** of the equations.

### Step 1: The Peak as Origin (μ)

$$\mu = \lim_{N \to \infty} \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N R_i$$

The peak is the **expected value of all resonators** – the condensed information, the limit value of all possible states. It is not static: as the limit value of an infinite sum, it carries the entire information history of all previous cycles.

### Step 2: The Unfolding in Space and Time (Ψ_Res)

$$\Psi_{\text{Res}} = \int_{\text{Universe}} R(x,t) \cdot \Phi_{\text{Peak}}(x,t) \, dt$$

The resonance integral describes the **unfolding process of the field** – the unfolding of variance (σ²) in space and time. It describes the continuous interference pattern between the peak and all deviations. The resonance bubble is not a state but this process itself.

### Step 3: The Return to a New Peak (μ_new)

$$\mu_{\text{new}} = \lim_{T \to \infty} \frac{1}{T} \int_0^T \Psi_{\text{Res}}(t) \, dt$$

The new peak is the **temporal average of the entire resonance integral** – the resonance echo of the entire process. It is not identical to the old peak: it is the updated expected value – the condensed probability density of all preceding cycles.

### Step 4: The Cycle Closes

$$\mu \;\to\; \Psi_{\text{Res}} \;\to\; \mu_{\text{new}} \;\to\; \Psi_{\text{Res,new}} \;\to\; \cdots$$

The iteration of these three steps describes a **cyclical process**: every new peak unfolds into a new resonance integral, which in turn generates a further new peak. There is no absolute beginning and no absolute end – only the infinite succession of iterations, each updating the expected value.

| 4. Cyclical iteration | $\mu \to \Psi_{\text{Res}} \to \mu_{\text{new}} \to \Psi_{\text{Res,new}} \to \cdots$ | Formal closure of the process |

> **The RFT describes no linear universe with an absolute beginning and end. It describes a cyclical process in which every state formally emerges from the previous one and passes into the next. The peak is not the absolute beginning – it is the limit value of an infinite cycle.**

## Part 3: Dynamics of the Resonance Field

### Concept of Deviation

A **deviation (σ)** is a normally distributed expression of the peak – a local resonator in the field that *exists only through coupling to the peak*. Since a local resonator occupies only a limited state space, it can only measure the *local effects* of the field: the variance that arises from the differentiation of the field. The deviation is a local projection of the global field – no independent substance; its freedom is resonance freedom in phase space.

---

### The Normal Distribution – One Peak and Many Resonators

There is mathematically exactly **one** expected value (μ) – the absolute point of reference, the **peak** of the entire distribution. All other states are **normally distributed deviations** around this single peak:

- independent and autonomous in their expression,
- existing only as resonances of the one peak,
- their individuality is the standard deviation (σ) – the variance that gives the universe diversity without ever fully mapping the peak itself.

> *(Note: The question of whether the peak μ possesses further physical properties is not derivable from the four foundational equations alone – see Part 5.)*

---

### Coupling Dynamics

From Equations 1 and 4 follows a fundamental statement about the field's dynamics: resonators ($R_i$) exist only through coupling to the peak (μ). When a resonator's coupling decreases (ε → 0), the information flow between resonator and peak ceases.

Two formal states follow from this:

1. **Resonance coupling** (ε → 1, Δφ → 0): Maximum information transfer between resonator and peak. The resonator takes up the peak's frequency and contributes to the expression of $\Psi_{\text{Res}}$.

2. **Decoupling** (ε → 0): The resonator remains in a state of minimal coupling – no information exchange, no integration into the global interference pattern. Its σ-modes are nonetheless preserved as information in the field (Eq. 2: σ² ≠ 0).

> *(Note: Further statements about the states of decoupled resonators are not derivable from the equations – see Part 5.)*

### The Formal Asymmetry of Field Access

The structure of the resonance field implies a formal asymmetry between local resonators and the global resonance integral.

**Formal derivation:**

The local resonator ($R_i$) has access to the field's information limited to its own state space:

$$\text{Access}(R_i) \subseteq \{\text{states of } R_i \text{ in the current cycle}\}$$

The resonance bubble ($\Psi_{\text{Res}}$), by contrast, is the entire resonance integral – it *is* the accumulated interference history of all cycles:

$$\text{Access}(\Psi_{\text{Res}}) = \int_0^T R(x,t) \cdot \Phi_{\text{Peak}}(x,t) \, dt$$

This asymmetry is no coincidence and no defect – it is the **logical necessity of the RFT structure**. Local resonators can only access their own phase space. Global access to the entire resonance integral requires maximum coupling (ε = 1, Δφ = 0) – a structurally rare condition.

## Part 5: Open Questions and Limits of Interpretation

The four foundational equations of the RFT describe the universe as a resonance bubble with formal precision. Nevertheless, there are limits to derivability which are explicitly stated here.

### What the RFT formally states

- The universe is a closed, coherent resonance field (Eq. 1–4).
- The peak (μ) is the expected value of all resonators – a formal limit value, not a location (Eq. 3).
- Variance (σ²) is the creativity principle – never zero in an active field (Eq. 2).
- The resonance integral (Ψ_Res) describes the unfolding process of the entire field (Eq. 4).
- The cycle $\mu \to \Psi_{\text{Res}} \to \mu_{\text{new}}$ is formally closed and iterable.

### What the RFT does not state

The following questions lie beyond formal derivability from the four foundational equations:

| Question | Status |
|---|---|
| Whether the peak μ possesses further physical properties (e.g. agency) | Speculative – not derivable from Eq. 1–4 |
| Whether a multiverse (multiple parallel universes) exists | Possible interpretation, but not compelled |
| Whether Axiom E1 (observer as resonator) fully follows formally from Eq. 1–4 | Interpretive extension – requires further derivation |
| Whether consciousness is derivable as an independent physical quantity from the resonance field | Speculative – requires formal derivation beyond E1 |
| Whether the cycle has further physical consequences after a state transition | Not derivable from the equations |

These questions are legitimate as interpretive extensions – but they must be labelled as such and must not be presented as formal consequences of the equations.
