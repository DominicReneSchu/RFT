## 3. Review: Effect of Axiom A8 on the Social Analysis Tool

**Result:** A8 (coupling wave velocity, RT-41, Sep 2026) is an irreducible postulate about the propagation velocity of phase waves at the speed of light c. For the social analysis tool, this yields:

| Aspect | Assessment |
|:--|:--|
| Direct change to structural patterns 7.1–7.13 | **No** – A8 describes physical propagation velocity, not social dynamics |
| Direct change to early indicators F1–F14 | **No** |
| Change to the core formulas (ε, PCI, K_ij) | **No** |
| Addition to the axiom table | **Yes** – A8 added as a row |
| Substantive implication for social analysis | Indirect: Social coupling also does not propagate instantaneously; network latency, information delay, and synchronization limits are consistent with A8 (finite propagation velocity as a boundary condition) |
| Relevance for falsification | Low – A8 is empirically anchored (c = 299 792 458 m/s); the social analogy (finite propagation) is heuristically not falsification-relevant at this level |

**Conclusion:** The social analysis tool remains unchanged in its core by A8. The axiom table is expanded by A8, and the note on the complete axiom system A1–A8 is added in Section 2.

## 4. Open Questions and Desiderata

1. **Empirical validation:** None of the quantities (PCI, K_ij, ε) has been empirically measured or statistically validated. Future research should develop operationalizations for measurability.
2. **Interrater reliability:** How consistently do different users estimate the same quantities in the same situation?
3. **Falsification studies:** Are there real cases in which the early indicators F1–F14 systematically show no predictive value?
4. **Source base for F10/F11:** Monologue dominance and resonance followership are well-described phenomena, but direct empirical studies on the RFT-specific constellations are lacking.
5. **A8 in social contexts:** The question of whether coupling changes in social networks have a measurable propagation time could be investigated empirically with computational network analyses (Twitter diffusion, information cascades).
