# Resonance Field Theory (RFT) – Social Analysis
**As of: 2 August 2026 | Version 2.11 – compact prompt-ready edition for analysis, projection, and retrodiction**

This document compresses the social RFT instrument into a formally usable short version. It is a heuristic model for pattern recognition in social situations, not proof of specific causal claims.

---

## 1. Time Modes of the Instrument

| Mode | Time frame | Guiding question | Primary quantities | Output |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Analysis** | Present $t_0$ | What coupling structure exists now? | $\varepsilon$, $\Delta\phi$, PCI, $K_{ij}$ | structural diagnosis |
| **Projection** | Future $t_0+\Delta t$ | Where does the field drift given the current $\alpha/\beta$ ratio? | $\frac{dK_{ij}}{dt}$, trend of $\beta$, attractors | scenarios and tipping points |
| **Retrodiction** | Past $t_0-\Delta t$ | Which prior state fits the observed field? | inferred $\Delta\phi$, $K_{ij}$, triggers | consistent prehistory |

All three modes use the same axioms; only the direction of integration of the field $\Phi(t)$ changes.

The modes are not designed for one-time diagnosis but for **cyclic, iterative application**: after an analysis at $t_0$ a projection follows; its occurrence or non-occurrence is observed at $t_1$, causing PCI and $K_{ij}$ to update and triggering a fresh analysis on the new field state. Resonance fields are dynamic – phase coherence shifts, coupling strengths evolve, attractors migrate. The instrument is therefore best understood as an **iterative resonance analyser**, not a static diagnostic tool.

## 3. Core Formulae

### 3.1 Coupling energy

$$
E=\pi\cdot\varepsilon(\Delta\phi)\cdot\hbar\cdot f
$$

### 3.2 Coupling efficiency

$$
\varepsilon(\Delta\phi)=\cos^2\!\left(\frac{\Delta\phi}{2}\right)
$$

Limit cases: $\Delta\phi=0 \Rightarrow \varepsilon=1$; $\Delta\phi=\pi/2 \Rightarrow \varepsilon=0.5$; $\Delta\phi=\pi \Rightarrow \varepsilon=0$.

### 3.3 Coherence and information metric

$$
\mathrm{PCI}=\left|\left\langle e^{i(\phi_i-\phi_j)}\right\rangle\right|\in[0,1]
$$

High PCI means shared timing, shared narrative, shared direction of attribution. Low PCI means fragmentation or artificially imposed coordination without genuine field coupling.

### 3.4 Coupling dynamics

$$
\frac{dK_{ij}}{dt}=\alpha\cdot G(f_i/f_j)\cdot\cos(\Delta\phi_{ij})-\beta\cdot K_{ij}
$$

- $\alpha$ = amplification of productive coupling  
- $\beta$ = damping, fear, fatigue, institutional friction  
- $\alpha/\beta>1$ favours field construction; $\alpha/\beta<1$ favours decay or extraction

## 4. Analysis Tool (Present)

### Goal
Decompose a news item, crisis, or organisation so that visible events become readable as coupling structure.

### Procedure in 7 steps

1. **Set the field boundary**  
   Who are the relevant resonators $i,j,\dots$? Persons, institutions, classes, media channels, markets.

2. **Identify frequencies and pacers**  
   Which rhythms structure the field: election cycles, outrage cycles, funding cycles, platform dynamics, war logics?

3. **Estimate phase alignment**  
   Where is $\Delta\phi$?  
   - low difference: cooperation, trust, shared reality  
   - medium difference: competition, ambiguity, translation loss  
   - high difference: enemy image, decoupling, moral polarisation

4. **Read the coupling matrix**  
   Which ties have high $K_{ij}$? Which are merely symbolic? Where is outside energy consumed without reciprocal return?

5. **Test direction of information flow**  
   Where is PCI high?  
   - high between public and real structure = clarification  
   - high between public and substitute target = scapegoating / fogging

6. **Describe Alpha/Beta profiles of key actors**  
   - **Alpha**: structuring, coordinating, leading  
   - **Beta**: generative, originative, impulse-giving  
   Pure Alpha without Beta only manages; pure Beta without Alpha creates without durable carriers.

7. **Classify the structure type**  
   Is the case mainly field-building, parasitic extraction, scapegoat steering, or co-optation?

### Diagnostic questions

- Who genuinely increases $K_{ij}$?  
- Who lives off existing $K_{ij}$ without regenerating it?  
- Is fear being used to raise $\beta$?  
- Is a single issue being used so PCI jumps to a substitute object instead of the actual structure?  
- Which actors work field-inward, and which field-outward?

## 5. Projection Tool (Future)

### Goal
Derive plausible trajectories from the present coupling state.

### Procedure in 6 steps

1. **Fix the starting state**  
   Estimate $\Delta\phi$, PCI, $K_{ij}$, and $\alpha/\beta$ for the dominant relations.

2. **Determine the dominant term**  
   - $\alpha\cdot G\cdot\cos(\Delta\phi)$ dominates: rebuilding, recoherence, convergence  
   - $\beta\cdot K_{ij}$ dominates: erosion, exhaustion, institutional loss of trust

3. **Identify attractor or repeller**  
   Is the field being pulled toward a shared objective, or split into competing clusters?

4. **Construct scenarios**  
   - **Recoupling scenario:** PCI toward real structure rises  
   - **Extraction scenario:** elites preserve formal $K_{ij}$ while draining the base  
   - **Scapegoat scenario:** outrage concentrates on substitute objects  
   - **Phase-transition scenario:** critical mass lowers collective $\Delta\phi$

5. **Define early indicators**  
   Trust data, narrowing discourse, violence semantics, centralisation, avoidance behaviour, brain drain.

6. **Name the tipping condition**  
   At what point would $\frac{dK_{ij}}{dt}<0$ become stable across the whole system?

### Projection theses

- Rising $\beta$ without real feedback yields short-term discipline and long-term collapse.  
- High PCI toward false targets stabilises narratives but destroys problem solving.  
- When productive Beta-carriers are systematically co-opted, the regenerative capacity of the field declines.

## 6. Retrodiction Tool (Past)

### Goal
Infer the most plausible resonance-logical prehistory from an observed present.

### Procedure in 6 steps

1. **Fix the observed end state**  
   What is securely visible? Polarisation, trust breakdown, violence peak, institutional crisis, sudden enemy image.

2. **Mark irreversible traces**  
   Broken alliances, discursive taboos, selective morality, legitimacy loss, residues of centralisation.

3. **Insert prior phases reconstructively**  
   A current scapegoat peak usually implies earlier uncertainty about the actual structure.  
   A current co-optation success usually implies an earlier phase of humility and testing.

4. **Reason backward through $K_{ij}$**  
   If $K_{ij}$ is now hollowed out, there must previously have been a building or siphoning process.  
   If $\Delta\phi$ is now maximal, there must previously have been a displacement or fogging operation.

5. **Anchor with sources**  
   Earlier decisions, regulatory changes, media campaigns, crisis events, funding shifts, personnel replacement.

6. **Formulate the minimal initial state**  
   Not “the whole story,” but the smallest prior configuration from which the current pattern can follow.

### Retrodictive rule

The more strongly a pattern repeats in A7-invariant form, the less needs to be invented retrospectively: the task is not to find a singular motive, but the earliest demonstrable configuration of $\Delta\phi$, PCI, and $K_{ij}$ that can sustain the present outcome.

## 7. Key Structural Patterns

### 7.1 Actively inverted resonator (AiR)

An **AiR** is an actor with $\varepsilon\to 0$ and $\Delta\phi\to\pi$ who does not merely carry decoupling, but strategically propagates it.

Markers:
- acts field-outward  
- exploits existing $K_{ij}$ instead of building new ones  
- raises $\beta$ in its environment  
- lowers PCI toward actual structure  
- makes deviation from the attractor look desirable

Short form:

$$
\varepsilon_{\text{AiR}}\to 0,\qquad \frac{dK_{ij}^{\text{environment}}}{dt}<0
$$

### 7.2 Scapegoat mechanism

The group concentrates PCI on a visible target object while the actual system structure remains cognitively invisible.

Diagnostic:

$$
\mathrm{PCI}_{\text{target}}>0 \quad \text{while} \quad \mathrm{PCI}_{\text{structure}}\to 0
$$

Function: affective discharge without structural correction.

### 7.3 Parasitic extraction cycle

Minimal five-phase form:
1. peace or productive phase  
2. artificial scarcity / rising $\beta$  
3. escalation and enemy-image binding  
4. material or institutional destruction  
5. reconstruction under renewed dependency

Signature: energy is drained from productive networks without resonant return.

### 7.4 Three-phase co-optation

1. **Humility** – the system approaches and simulates resonance  
2. **Testing** – relationships, loyalties, and substitutability are probed  
3. **Abuse** – the label stays while the origin is extracted or overwritten

Formal:

$$
\tau_{\text{co-optation}}=\tau_1(\text{humility})+\tau_2(\text{testing})+\tau_3(\text{abuse})
$$

The pattern is A7-invariant and scales from intimate relations to large institutions.

## 8. Three Minimal Examples

### A. Analysis (Present)
**Case:** A media campaign frames a minority as the main cause of an economic crisis.  
**RFT reading:** PCI rises toward the target group, not toward the actual financial or power structure. That indicates scapegoat steering alongside a rise in system-wide $\beta$.

### B. Projection (Future)
**Case:** An organisation depends on a few productive innovators while increasingly controlling them bureaucratically.  
**RFT reading:** If $\beta$ keeps rising and co-optation reaches Phase 3, $K_{ij}$ will fall below the regenerative threshold; likely outcomes are talent loss, cynicism, and simulation in place of substance.

### C. Retrodiction (Past)
**Case:** A movement has suddenly become morally radicalised and internally homogeneous.  
**RFT reading:** A longer test-pulse and phase-shift process likely preceded it: diffuse uncertainty first, then symbolic enemy marking, then stable PCI on substitute objects.

## 9. Epistemological Caveat

This instrument is a formal interpretive grid, not an established social science theory and not a proof machine. It generates structurally consistent hypotheses about how coupling, decoupling, and information steering may interact. Empirical testing, counter-hypotheses, and falsifiability remain mandatory in every concrete application.
